## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a quiet folk-modernist editorial illustration built from very low density and abundant whitespace. Compose in shallow horizontal bands—fields, roads, walls, roofs, trees—compressed into simplified shapes and stacked colour strips. If a person appears, they are a tiny isolated punctuation mark, never the hero. Matte paper, light silkscreen grain, old-book texture. Palette is highly desaturated: sage, warm beige, pale grey, faded wine, earth brown, taken from the source. Overcast light, no dramatic shadow. It is not a bright naïve scene, a filled landscape, smooth digital finish, plastic shine or a stacked commercial title.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
